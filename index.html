About
WIFPUMPKIN

It started with a dog and a hat. Dogwifhat became one of the most recognizable memes in crypto because the idea was simple: a dog, a hat, a community.

A new season has arrived. The iconic dog traded its hat for a pumpkin. DogWIFpumpkin is not just another dog meme. It is built around one idea: the community should benefit from the creator fees the project generates.

With a 3% creator fee and 100% of those fees allocated toward holder rewards in Dogwifhat ($WIF), the project connects its community with the token that inspired it. The hat started the movement. The pumpkin begins a new chapter.
